Convert to TensorRT

Builds an FP16 or FP32 TensorRT engine from with Ultralytics' export(format="engine"). It runs in the background, one conversion at a time, and usually takes a few minutes on the Jetson.

The engine's input size is fixed. Ultralytics rounds each side up to a multiple of 32: for 1280 × 720 images, height 720 becomes 736.
